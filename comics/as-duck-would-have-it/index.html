Read at Stanford Magazine

How I learned to swim in the Stanford pond.

My student spotlight comic, “As Duck Would Have It,” made a splash in the Stanford Alumni Magazine in Fall 2024. The story reflects my experience learning to swim in the Stanford pond, both literally and figuratively, as I reconciled my passion for art with the imposter syndrome that came with studying computer science.
